💨 The 4-7-8 Breathing Technique

            
A natural tranquilizer for the nervous system to quickly reduce panic and performance anxiety.

            
    
              
  1. Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 seconds.
    2. 
              
  2. Hold your breath for a count of 7 seconds.
    3. 
              
  3. Exhale completely through your mouth making a whoosh sound for 8 seconds.
    4. 
              
  4. Repeat the cycle 4 times during intense stress spikes.
    5. 
            

          


          

            
🎯 The Pomodoro Study Technique

            
Helps combat study fatigue and prevents time management burnout.

            
    
              
  • Choose a single topic to focus on.
    • 
              
  • Set a timer for 25 minutes and study with zero distractions.
    • 
              
  • Take a 5-minute active break (walk, stretch, hydrate).
    • 
              
  • Every 4 pomodoros, take a longer 20-30 minute break to recharge.
    •
